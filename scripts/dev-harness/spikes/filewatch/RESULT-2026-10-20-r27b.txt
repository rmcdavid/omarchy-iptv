R27b (2026-10-20, D-SRC-48), measure.sh <helper> r27b, Quickshell 0.3.1 / Qt 6.11.2, nested headless cage on a private D-Bus (private-bus.sh), one FileView declared as Service.qml's stateFile is.

THE QUESTION. Does ONE re-read after our own `saved`, made when a fileChanged came while our write was out (the board's candidate mitigation), ever recover a change another program wrote that the read R26a makes before the NEXT save (here `laterRead`, one reload a second later) does not already recover?

Each trial: a python writer (an atomic rename like the helper's, or an in-place write) writes change C=c<N> DELAY ms after it starts while the probe saves own<N> (PAD bytes, so the write lasts long enough to be landed in). Every signal is stamped on the wall clock the writer stamps too, so C@ says where C landed against our write: before-set (before our setText), during (between our setText and our `saved`), after-saved. C-survived: the file holds C when the trial settles. view-saw-C: a load carrying C reached the view on its own (no mitigation, before the later read). mitigation: asked when the probe made the re-read, C when that read loaded C. later-read: C when the read a second later loaded C. recovers-new: the cell that matters -- the mitigation loaded C and the later read did not.

VERDICT. 608 trials, 25 of them with C landed DURING our write (196 before it began, 387 after it was saved). recovers-new: 0 of 608.
  (R28e, 2026-10-20: this line read "30 of them" and the second bullet "21 of the 30" until round 1's review recounted the raw lines below: `grep -c 'C@during'` is 25, of which `C-survived:no` 20 and `C-survived:yes` 5; 196 + 25 + 387 = 608. The headline and the verdict were right.)
  - The mitigation's read loaded C in 4 trials (all four C landed during our write and survived), and in all 4 the later read loaded C too: the two reads read the same disk, and between them nothing of ours writes it (R27a: no write without a read).
  - 20 of the 25 during-trials LOST C: it landed before our rename and the rename replaced it. No read of any kind recovers that -- it is D-SRC-48 itself, on every tree, and so are the before-set trials (76 in run 2, 120 in run 1), C landing before our write began.
  - 9 trials ended with C on disk and NEVER seen by the view on its own (4 during, 5 after-saved; one of them with no mitigation at all): the later read loaded C in all 9.
So the mitigation changes only WHEN memory learns of a surviving C -- at once, instead of at the next save's read (or the next foreign change) -- and recovers nothing that read does not. Nothing was built (R27b: build nothing and say so).
